Emergency Kit Guide

Essential items recommended for a standard household emergency survival kit.

Drinking Water

Store at least 3 litres of water per person per day for drinking and sanitation.

Non-Perishable Food

Keep ready-to-eat canned food, energy bars, and dried items that require no cooking.

First Aid Supplies

Bandages, antiseptic wipes, sterile gauze, scissors, tape, and pain relievers.

Torch and Batteries

Reliable flashlights with extra batteries or hand-crank options for power failures.

Power Bank

Fully charged portable power banks to keep communication devices operational.

Important Documents

Copies of ID proofs, insurance papers, and contact numbers kept in a waterproof pouch.

Basic Medicines

Personal prescription medications, oral rehydration salts, and basic fever reducers.

Whistle

Useful for signaling rescuers if you are trapped or unable to call out loudly.

Immediate Actions

  • Stay calm and follow official announcements.
  • Move away from damaged structures or flooded zones.
  • Keep your emergency kit or ID documents accessible.
  • Check on family members and neighbors if safe.
